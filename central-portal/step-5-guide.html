STEP 5 · PRODUCT V1

Make your project usable

Connect your saved model, controller or optimiser to an interface. A user should enter inputs, run your method and understand the result.

Deadline: 8 November 2026, 11:59:59 PM IST.

Continue from Step 4

  • Read your published feedback and address relevant earlier issues.
  • Create step-5-product-v1 from the branch containing your completed Step 4 work.
  • Keep the saved method, preprocessing, feature order and earlier comparison evidence available.
  • Use your own project requirements. SC11 water quality is a teaching example; its fields and code are not suitable for every project.

What we expect

  1. A working interface: let a user provide the inputs needed by your project.
  2. Actual inference or optimisation: call your saved method and show its output. Keep training outside the prediction button.
  3. Input validation: explain missing, invalid and out-of-range input without crashing.
  4. Readable output: include labels, units, meaning and limitations. Show unplaced requests, violated constraints or uncertainty when relevant.
  5. Test evidence: record normal, boundary, invalid-input, missing-model/service and UI-versus-code checks.
  6. Reproducibility: document dependencies, entry file, start command and local or hosted access.
  7. Documentation: complete docs/STEP-5.md with actual observations, screenshots, addressed feedback and member contributions.
  8. Git evidence: push the branch and meaningful commit before submitting the portal form.

Adapt the output to your project

  • Climate: cooling, fan and energy action from room conditions.
  • Washing: cycle/settings from fabric, load and dirt inputs.
  • Academic support: risk indicator and supportive action from documented indicators.
  • Traffic: signal plan and simulation results from a traffic scenario.
  • Timetable: schedule, unplaced requests and constraint results.
  • Route: route, distance and constraint results.
  • Irrigation: irrigation recommendation from soil/weather inputs.
  • Maintenance: failure warning and priority from machine measurements.
  • Credit: decision-support output with error limits from permitted features.
  • Energy: appliance schedule, cost and constraint results.

A local demonstration is acceptable. Hosting is optional. Report observed performance without claiming real-world savings or safety that your evidence has not established.
